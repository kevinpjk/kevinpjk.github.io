Welcome! I'm a third year PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Toronto.
I am advised by Prof. [Tovi Grossman](https://www.tovigrossman.com/) at the [Dynamic Graphics Group](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/) (DGP) lab and Prof. [Yan Chen](https://chensivan.github.io/).

My research interests lie in human-computer interaction, artificial intelligence, and their collaboration. My past works have focused on aligning AI support with human capabilities to build harmonious mixed-initiative and AI-proactive workflows for automation, programming, and ideation.

Before PhD, I received my MSc. degree from the University of Toronto where I was advised by Prof. [Tovi Grossman](https://www.tovigrossman.com/) and Prof. [Yan Chen](https://chensivan.github.io/), and B.S. degree with high distinction in Computer Science and Psychology from the University of Michigan, where I had the pleasure to work at the Computational HCI lab with Prof. [Nikola Banovic](http://www.nikolabanovic.net/index.html).
