Welcome! I'm a 5th year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of Toronto.
I am advised by Prof. [Tovi Grossman](https://www.tovigrossman.com/) at the [Dynamic Graphics Group](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/) (DGP) lab and Prof. [Yan Chen](https://chensivan.github.io/).

My research interests lie in human-computer interaction, artificial intelligence, and their intersection. My past works have focused on aligning AI support with human capabilities to build harmonious mixed-initiative and AI-proactive workflows for automation, programming, and ideation.

## Research Keyword Categories

### Mixed-Initiative Systems
Design and evaluation of systems where humans and AI share control — exploring the trade-offs of proactive AI support, user agency, and workflow disruption in programming and task automation contexts.

### Human-AI Collaboration
Building AI-powered tools that augment human cognitive capabilities — from working memory-aware proactive assistants on wearable devices, to collaborative ideation systems grounded in scientific literature, to analytics dashboards for understanding collaborative learning.

### Neurosymbolic Programming
Combining neural language understanding with symbolic program synthesis — enabling end-users to create expressive web automation programs that handle diverse logical conditions and semantic content filtering through programming-by-demonstration.
